Why Stuck Nation?

We Live in the Woods.
We Play in the Woods.

And sometimes, we get stuck in the woods.

Stuck Nation started with three people who genuinely enjoy being outdoors, working on machines and spending time on the trails. We've been the ones buried in mud, broken on the trail and trying to figure out how we're getting a rig back home.

We wanted to take that experience and use it to help other people enjoy their time out there as much as we enjoy ours.

Break something. Get stuck. Fix it. Recover it. Then get back to having fun.
